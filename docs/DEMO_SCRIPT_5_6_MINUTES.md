# Demo Script (5–6 minutes)

## Goal
Show the HRMS key workflows end-to-end: dashboard → employee modules → admin approvals → feedback/notifications.

## Assumptions
- Database is seeded (so demo credentials exist).
- You can log in successfully.

## Seeded Accounts
- **Super Admin**: `admin@hrms.ph` / `password`
- **Employee (pick one)**: `juan.delacruz@hrms.ph` / `password`

---

## Minute-by-minute walkthrough

### 0:00 – 0:30 | Login + Landing
1. Open the app and **log in as Employee**.
2. Go to **Dashboard**.
   - Point out the summary/cards/overview area (whatever is shown on `dashboard`).

**What you should say:** “This dashboard gives the employee quick visibility of their current HR status and pending items.”

---

### 0:30 – 1:45 | Attendance (time-in/time-out)
1. Navigate to **Attendance**.
2. Choose **time-in** flow:
   - If there’s a button/action for time-in, click it.
3. Choose **time-out** flow:
   - Click the time-out action.
4. Refresh and/or observe the attendance row for today.

**What you should say:** “Attendance updates are immediate and reflect in the employee records.”

---

### 1:45 – 2:30 | Leaves (create a request)
1. Go to **Leaves**.
2. Create a **new leave request** (select type, start/end date).
3. Submit.
4. Confirm it appears in the leaves list as **pending**.

**What you should say:** “Employees can request leaves; requests move into a pending state for admin review.”

---

### 2:30 – 3:20 | Timesheets (view “my”)
1. Go to **Timesheets**.
2. Click **My Timesheets** (or filter to “my”).
3. Open one timesheet entry.
4. Point out fields like total hours, status (pending/approved), and the week range.

**What you should say:** “Timesheets are organized per week and tracked through a review/approval lifecycle.”

---

### 3:20 – 4:00 | Violations (view)
1. Go to **Violations**.
2. Open any violation entry shown for the employee.
3. Point out level/offense/status/date.

**What you should say:** “Violations provide a record of disciplinary actions and current status.”

---

### 4:00 – 4:30 | Performance (view)
1. Go to **Performance**.
2. Open **My performance** or the list view.
3. Point out score/rating/feedback and the review period.

**What you should say:** “Performance reviews show score, rating, and feedback over time.”

---

### 4:30 – 5:30 | Notifications (employee perspective)
1. Go to **Notifications**.
2. Click **read all** OR open one notification.
3. Confirm the notification(s) change state (e.g., unread → read).

**What you should say:** “Notifications keep employees informed about approvals, updates, and actions.”

---

### 5:30 – 6:00 | Switch to Admin + Approve
1. Log out.
2. Log in as **Super Admin**: `admin@hrms.ph` / `password`.
3. Go to **Leaves** and approve the pending leave request created earlier.
4. Go to **Timesheets** and approve/reject one pending timesheet if available.
5. (Optional if time allows) Go to **Requests** and approve/reject a pending request.

**What you should say:** “Admins review pending employee submissions and update status via approve/reject actions.”

---

## Demo “sanity checks” (quick)
- Attendance row shows **time-in/time-out** updated for today.
- Leaves list shows **pending** after submitting.
- Timesheets shows correct **week + status**.
- Notifications show **state change** after marking read.
- Admin view shows **status updated** after approvals.
